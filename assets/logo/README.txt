Official Phantom Siita artwork bundled with the app
===================================================

Files
-----
phantomsiita-wordmark.svg      the group wordmark (137 x 24.6)
phantomsiita-butterfly-01.svg  wide butterfly, used for the panel ornaments and
                               the menu row icon (149 x 82.5)
phantomsiita-butterfly-02.svg  taller butterfly, used for the sorter loader
                               (159 x 146)
phantomsiita-pattern.png       the pale butterfly paper texture used as the
                               panel background (228 x 73, repeated at 100px
                               like the official stylesheet does)
phantomsiita-drop.svg          the animated red drip that hangs from the right
                               edge of the header in black mode only (160 x 484,
                               a Lottie style SVG whose SMIL animation plays
                               inside an <img> in Chromium)

         #logo--official, #butterfly01, #butterfly02; and

Why they are here
-----------------
They are bundled at the project owner's explicit request, so the header, the
menu title, the entry gate, the panel ornaments and the loader can show the real
brand artwork instead of plain text or placeholder shapes. Everything else in
assets/pixel is generated by tools/gen-pixel-assets.mjs and belongs to this
project.

The butterflies are drawn through a CSS mask (see .mark in css/style.css) so
they take `currentColor` and can sit on the pink paper panels as well as on the
dark ones.

Note that the official terms of use (https://phantomsiita.com/feature/terms)
do not grant permission to copy or redistribute site content. Publishing a fan
tool that carries these marks is the owner's call and carries the usual
trademark and copyright risk; it can also look like official branding even
though the app is not affiliated with the group or its label.

To remove them again: delete this folder, drop the usages (index.html brand
image, js/views/menu.js title + row icon, js/main.js gate, js/views/common.js
panel ornaments, js/views/vote.js loader) and go back to text titles and the
generated pixel sprites.

Not to be confused with the favicon
-----------------------------------
The browser tab icon is assets/pixel/heart.svg and must stay that way: the owner
asked for it explicitly and it is recorded as a locked decision in AGENTS.md.
